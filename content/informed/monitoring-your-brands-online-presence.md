Conversations about your brand happen whether you listen or not. Monitoring makes sure you hear them first.

## What to track

- Volume of mentions across social, news and forums.
- Sentiment, read by people who know the language and context.
- Share of voice against your closest competitors.
- Recurring themes: what people praise, and what they complain about.

## From data to decisions

Every listening report should answer three questions: what happened, why does it matter, and what should we do next. That is the thinking behind Bluetrain Informed.
