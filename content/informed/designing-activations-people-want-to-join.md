A branded tent and a sound system will draw a crowd. Turning that crowd into participants takes design.

## Make the first step obvious

If someone has to ask what is happening, you have lost them. A clear, visible challenge or prize brings people in.

## Design for spectators

Most people at an activation are watching. Make the action visible and fun to watch, and the crowd grows on its own.

## Close the loop

Every activation should hand off to something: a promotion entry, a WhatsApp follow-up, a sample, a reason to come back.
