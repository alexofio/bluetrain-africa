Cost per acquisition is cost per click divided by conversion rate. Lower the CPC and CPA falls, but only if conversion holds.

## Why cheap clicks mislead

Broad audiences and placements often bring cheaper clicks from people who were never going to buy. CPA rises even as CPC falls.

## Where to look first

- Landing page speed and clarity, especially on mobile.
- Audience and keyword quality.
- Conversion tracking: are you measuring the right action?

Optimise for the outcome the business pays for. Clicks are only a step on the way.
