Consumer promotions live or die on how easy it is to enter. Before creative, decide how people will take part.

## USSD

Works on basic phones and needs no data or app. Best for mass-market promotions where reach matters more than richness. Keep menus short.

## WhatsApp

Familiar and conversational. Good for entries that need a photo or receipt, and for follow-up messages. Plan for opt-in and message templates early.

## Microsite

The most control over look, feel and data. Best for smartphone audiences and campaigns that need storytelling, galleries or leaderboards.

Many of our larger promotions combine two: USSD for reach, a microsite for winners and content.
