Influencer marketing often starts with a list of big names. The better question is: who does your audience already listen to?

## Trust over reach

A creator with a smaller, engaged community can move more people than a celebrity with a passive audience. Look at comments, not just counts.

## Brief the outcome

Tell creators what you need people to think, feel or do. Then let them say it in their own voice. Scripts kill credibility.

## Measure what matters

Track saves, shares, replies, clicks and sales alongside reach. People influence people, and the evidence is in what they do next.
