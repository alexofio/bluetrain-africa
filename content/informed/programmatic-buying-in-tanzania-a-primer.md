Programmatic advertising buys ad space automatically, targeting audiences across websites and apps rather than booking placements one by one.

## Where it fits

Use it to extend reach beyond search and social, retarget visitors, and reach mobile audiences in apps. It complements, rather than replaces, the core channels.

## Questions to ask

- Which inventory will my ads appear on, and how is brand safety handled?
- How is viewability measured?
- What does reporting look like, and how often?
